What we build

Everything a local business needs to be found online, built properly and kept up to date.

  • Shop websites

    A clean one-page site with your photos, opening hours, map, price list and buttons to call or WhatsApp you.

    For groceries, bakeries, salons, tailors, hardware and phone shops.

  • Clinic and pharmacy sites

    Doctor lists with channelling days and times, a call-to-book button, and schedule changes whenever you send them.

    For medical centres, dental clinics, pharmacies and opticians.

  • Full business websites

    • Up to 6 pages on your own .lk domain
    • Business email address
    • Built to load fast on any phone

    For larger clinics, supermarkets, schools and hotels.

  • Google Maps listing

    We set up or fix your Google Business Profile, so you show up with the right hours, photos and phone number.

    Included in most packages.

  • Care and updates

    New prices, offers or holiday hours: send us a WhatsApp and we update your site. Every few months you get a report on how many people visited and called.

    Part of every yearly plan.

How we work

From a first chat to a live website in four steps. Most shop sites are ready within a week.

  1. 1

    We visit you

    A free, short chat at your shop. We note what you sell and take photos.

  2. 2

    We design and build

    Your site, written in the languages your customers use.

  3. 3
