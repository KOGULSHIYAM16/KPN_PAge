How payment works

  • The first payment is made before your site goes live.
  • Pay by bank transfer, LankaQR or cash. You always get a receipt.
  • Yearly care is due on your site's anniversary. Business and above can pay it in two parts.
  • We remind you 30 days before, with your latest visitor numbers.
  • If care is unpaid after 30 days, the site is paused, not deleted.

Questions we often get

I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need a website?

Facebook pages often don't show up when people search on Google. A website does, and it links to your Facebook page too, so you get both.

Do I need a computer or any technical skill?

No. We handle everything. When something changes, send us a WhatsApp message and we update the site.

How long does it take?

Most one-page sites are ready within a week of our visit. A full Premium website takes about two to three weeks.

Will my website be fast?

Yes. We build light pages and host them on a global network with a server in Colombo, so your site opens quickly on mobile data anywhere in Sri Lanka.

Can I move up to a bigger package later?

Yes. You pay only the difference in the first payment, and your yearly care changes to the new package from then on.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across Sri Lanka. For shops near us we visit in person; for others we work over WhatsApp and phone.
